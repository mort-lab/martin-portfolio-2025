Trimio in its initial phase was my first entrepreneurship as Founder & Fullstack Developer, focused on solving real payment management problems for barbershops and tattoo studios. This stage was fundamental for market validation and gathering direct feedback that would eventually evolve into Trimio Learn.

## Key Features

- **Simplified Billing Management**: Intuitive interface to register services and generate charges.
- **Digital Payments**: Complete Stripe integration to accept cards and digital methods.
- **Stripe Connect**: System for each barbershop to handle their own payments independently.
- **Business Dashboard**: Basic metrics and analytics for small entrepreneurs.
- **Customer Management**: Simple database for service history per client.

## Technology Stack

- **Framework**: **Next.js** with **Server Actions** for modern full-stack development.
- **Database**: **PostgreSQL** for reliable and scalable storage.
- **Payments**: **Stripe** with **Stripe Connect** for distributed processing.
- **Language**: **TypeScript** for type-safe development from the start.
- **Styling**: **TailwindCSS** for fast and consistent UI.
- **Hosting**: Optimized deployment for small businesses.

## Technical Implementation

### Modern Next.js Architecture

- **App Router** with the latest Next.js 13+ features.
- **Server Actions** for secure mutations without separate API routes.
- **Server Components** for optimized rendering and better SEO.
- **Strategic Client Components** for specific interactivity.

### Robust Payment System

- **Stripe Connect Express** for rapid barbershop onboarding.
- **Payment Intent API** for secure transactions and PCI compliance.
- **Webhook handling** for payment confirmations and status updates.
- **Refund management** for exceptional cases and customer satisfaction.

### Optimized Database

- **PostgreSQL schema** designed for simple but effective relationships.
- **Prisma ORM** (considered) vs **raw SQL** for critical operations.
- **Indexing strategy** for frequent customer history queries.
- **Automated backup** for business data protection.

### Sector-Specific UX

- **Mobile-first design** considering smartphone use during services.
- **Quick actions** for fast charges between clients.
- **Basic offline capability** for environments with limited connectivity.
- **Print integration** for physical receipts when necessary.

## How It Works

1. **Barbershop Registration**: Simplified onboarding with basic business verification.
2. **Service Setup**: Configuration of service list with custom prices.
3. **Customer Management**: Quick customer registration with basic history.
4. **Billing Process**: Streamlined interface to generate and process payments.
5. **Daily Dashboard**: Summary of income, services and basic metrics.

## Market Validation

### Direct Feedback from Real Barbershops

During this phase, I personally visited over 20 barbershops and tattoo studios to:

- **Understand real pain points** of the sector in payment management.
- **Observe current workflows** and identify improvement opportunities.
- **Test prototypes** directly with owners and employees.
- **Gather feedback** on pricing, features and usability.

### Key Insights Discovered

- **Need for simplicity** over complex features.
- **Importance of low commissions** for tight sector margins.
- **Resistance to change** but openness if value is clear.
- **Demand for educational tools** to grow their businesses.

## Achievements and Metrics

- **Successful validation** of problem and initial solution-market fit.
- **15+ pilot barbershops** using the platform during testing.
- **90% feedback rate** from active users providing valuable input.
- **Zero critical incidents** in payment handling during testing phase.
- **Solid foundation** for evolution towards more ambitious educational model.

## Challenges and Learnings

### Technical Challenges
- **Compliance with regulations** for payments and data protection.
- **Edge cases** in sector-specific payment flows.
- **Performance optimization** for basic mobile devices.
- **User education** about digitalization benefits.

### Business Learnings
- **Importance of product-market fit** before technical scaling.
- **Value of direct feedback** vs. assumptions about users.
- **Need for evolution** towards higher value-added models.
- **Market timing** and adoption in traditional sectors.

## Evolution towards Trimio Learn

The insights from this initial phase were fundamental for the pivot towards Trimio Learn:

### Identified Needs
- **Education and growth** as greater pain point than just payments.
- **Community and networking** among sector professionals.
- **Expertise monetization** of experienced barbers and tattoo artists.
- **More complete platform** that goes beyond simple transactions.

### Reused Technical Foundation
- **Robust payment architecture** ready to scale.
- **UI patterns** validated with real sector users.
- **Extensible database schema** towards educational functionalities.
- **Stripe integration** prepared for more complex models.

## Impact on Professional Development

- **Experience as founder** from ideation to implementation.
- **Market validation** with lean startup methodologies.
- **User research** in traditional non-tech sectors.
- **Product management** balancing features vs. simplicity.
- **Technical leadership** in end-to-end own project.

## Legacy and Transition

Although initial Trimio was eventually absorbed by Trimio Learn, it established:

- **Credibility in the sector** for future initiatives.
- **Network of early adopters** who became users of the new platform.
- **Learning foundation** about real market needs.
