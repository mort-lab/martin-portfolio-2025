GetPolara.ai is an AI-powered video generation platform I developed as a freelance project through Upwork. The platform enables users to create professional videos using advanced AI models, with a complete system of authentication, credit-based payments and automated subscription management.

## Key Features

- **AI Video Generation**: Integration with cutting-edge APIs to create high-quality videos.
- **Credit System**: Flexible business model based on credit consumption per generated video.
- **Complete Authentication**: Robust system for registration, login and user account management.
- **Automated Payments**: Complete Stripe integration for credit purchases and subscriptions.
- **Expiration Management**: Automated system for renewals and email notifications.

## Technology Stack

- **Backend**: Developed with **Laravel** leveraging its mature ecosystem and integrated tools.
- **Database**: **PostgreSQL** on **NeonDB** for scalable and efficient storage.
- **Payment Processing**: **Stripe** with credit system and recurring subscriptions.
- **AI & Video**: **Replicate API** for access to state-of-the-art video generation models.
- **Email Marketing**: Automated notification and email marketing system.
- **Hosting**: Optimized implementation for handling heavy multimedia files.

## Technical Implementation

### Laravel Architecture

- **MVC Pattern** with controllers organized by specific functionalities.
- **Eloquent ORM** for efficient management of complex relationships between users, credits and videos.
- **Laravel Queues** for asynchronous processing of videos and heavy tasks.
- **API Resources** for consistent and well-structured JSON responses.

### Credit System

- **Flexible model** where each video type consumes different amounts of credits.
- **Atomic transactions** ensuring consistency in purchases and consumption.
- **Detailed history** of credit usage and video generation.
- **Automatic alerts** when credits are about to run out.

### AI Integration

- **Replicate API** for access to multiple video generation models.
- **Queue system** to handle AI jobs that can take several minutes.
- **Webhook handling** to receive video completion notifications.
- **Robust error handling** for generation failures or external APIs.

### Payment System

- **Stripe Checkout** for optimized payment experience.
- **Stripe Webhooks** for secure payment confirmation.
- **Failure handling** and automatic payment retries.
- **Automatic billing** and international tax management.

## How It Works

1. **User Registration**: Simplified onboarding with automatic email verification.
2. **Credit Purchase**: Selection of credit packages with tiered pricing.
3. **Video Generation**: Intuitive interface to configure parameters and generate videos.
4. **Processing**: Queue system processes AI jobs in the background.
5. **Delivery**: Automatic notification when video is ready for download.
