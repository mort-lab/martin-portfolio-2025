During my work as Shopify Backend Developer at Línea Gráfica, I developed a specialized system for massive migrations from PrestaShop to Shopify. The project involved processing hundreds of thousands of records including products, customers, historical orders and complex relationships between entities.

## Key Features

- **Massive Data Migration**: Efficient processing of large volumes of business information.
- **Shopify Private Apps**: Development of custom applications for specific functionalities.
- **Bidirectional Synchronization**: Maintaining consistency between legacy systems and Shopify.
- **Integrity Validation**: Automatic verification of migrated data and inconsistency detection.
- **Rollback & Recovery**: Backup systems to revert migrations in case of critical errors.

## Technology Stack

- **Shopify APIs**: **GraphQL API** and **REST API** for maximum efficiency in operations.
- **Backend**: **PHP** for data processing and complex business logic.
- **Database**: **MySQL** for temporary storage and migration logs.
- **PrestaShop Integration**: **PrestaShop API** and direct database access.
- **ETL Pipeline**: Extract, Transform, Load processes optimized for large volumes.
- **Shopify Private Apps**: Custom applications with elevated permissions.

## Technical Implementation

### Migration Architecture

- **ETL Pipeline** designed to process data in optimized batches.
- **Queue system** for asynchronous handling of heavy operations.
- **Robust error handling** with detailed logs and automatic recovery.
- **Intelligent rate limiting** to avoid Shopify API throttling.

### Entity Processing

#### Product Migration
- **Extraction** of complete catalogs including variants, images and metafields.
- **Transformation** of data structures between PrestaShop and Shopify.
- **Category mapping** and taxonomies between different systems.
- **SEO optimization** maintaining existing URLs and metadata.

#### Customer Migration
- **History preservation** of purchases and user preferences.
- **Secure encryption** of sensitive data during the process.
- **Email validation** and automatic duplicate cleanup.
- **Address migration** with geocoding for better UX.

#### Historical Orders
- **Complete import** of transactional history for analytics.
- **Total recalculation** considering differences in tax systems.
- **State preservation** and existing shipment tracking.
- **Integration with external fulfillment** services.

### Shopify GraphQL Specialization

- **Bulk Operations** for efficient processing of large datasets.
- **Admin API mutations** optimized for batch operations.
- **Webhook management** for real-time post-migration synchronization.
- **Metafield strategies** to preserve custom PrestaShop data.

## How It Works

1. **Pre-Migration Analysis**: Complete audit of PrestaShop data and planning.
2. **Environment Setup**: Setup of private apps and permissions in Shopify.
3. **Data Extraction**: Secure backup and export from PrestaShop.
4. **Transformation**: Mapping and conversion of data to Shopify format.
5. **Gradual Loading**: Import by batches with continuous validation.
6. **Final Verification**: Exhaustive testing and integrity comparison.
